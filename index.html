How does Akwaaba Guide choose the Top 10 in each list?

Every list follows a published four-step method: we set the criteria first, gather public and first-hand evidence, score each candidate against the same criteria, and have an editor review the result before it is published. Each article shows the date it was last updated.

Can a business pay to be included in a Top 10 list?

No. Rankings are never sold. Businesses can submit information for review, and any sponsored placement is clearly labelled and kept separate from the editorial ranking.

Which cities and regions in Ghana do you cover?

We cover all 16 regions of Ghana, with in-depth guides for Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, Tamale, Cape Coast and Tema.

How often are the lists updated?

Each article shows a last-updated date and is reviewed on a regular schedule so that readers see current information.
